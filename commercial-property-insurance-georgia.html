Coverage Review

Coverage Options We Review Together

Availability, terms, and limits vary by market and risk. I’ll explain what is included in each offered option and where separate coverage may be needed.

  • ◆
    Building ProtectionReview limits for covered damage to the structure, with valuation, covered causes of loss, deductibles, and exclusions confirmed for the option offered.
  • ◆
    Contents & Tenant ImprovementsReview business equipment, furniture, inventory, and improvements you are responsible for under your lease.
  • ◆
    Business Income / Rental IncomeWhen offered, income coverage may respond after covered property damage interrupts operations or rental income, subject to policy triggers, limits, waiting periods, and restoration terms.
  • ◆
    Equipment BreakdownMay be available by endorsement or separate coverage for eligible equipment breakdown losses. Standard property coverage should not be assumed to include it.
  • ◆
    Ordinance or LawReview available coverage for eligible demolition, undamaged portions, and increased construction costs following a covered loss and applicable code requirements.
  • ◆
    Liability OptionsGeneral liability is a separate coverage consideration. An eligible business owners policy or package may combine property and liability; a property-only policy should not be assumed to include both.

One Package or Separate Policies?

An eligible small business may qualify for a business owners policy combining property, liability, and income coverages. Other buildings and operations may need a commercial package or separate policies.

Eligibility and the coverages actually included must be confirmed for your specific property and business.

Flood & Water Need Their Own Review

Do not assume a property policy covers every water-related loss. I’ll review flood, water backup, exclusions, and sublimits separately from wind or hurricane protection.

Common Questions

Georgia Commercial Property FAQ

What information do I need to start?

Have your contact and business or ownership details, property address, state, operations, occupancy, construction, roof age if known, coverage needs, and insurance history ready. Use Not sure where available. No documents are required to begin.

Do business tenants need their own property insurance?

A building owner’s policy should not be assumed to cover a tenant’s equipment, inventory, improvements, or lost business income. Tell me what you lease and your lease requirements so I can review your needs.

Is general liability included in commercial property insurance?

Not automatically. A qualifying business owners policy or package may combine property and liability coverages. I’ll review whether liability is included or needs a separate policy.

Does income coverage apply to every interruption?

No. Business income and rental income coverage depend on the policy’s covered-loss triggers, limits, waiting periods, restoration provisions, and exclusions. I’ll review the terms offered for your operations.

Can you review vacant buildings or properties under renovation?

Yes. Disclose current occupancy and describe the work, contractor, and expected completion. The available program and any coverage restrictions depend on the property and market.

Does wind coverage include flood or storm surge?

Do not assume it does. Wind damage and flooding are different exposures. Separate flood coverage may be needed; actual policy terms determine protection.

What happens after I send my request?

You’ll receive an acknowledgment email, plus a confirmation text if you opt in. I review requests during business hours and follow up about available markets or additional information. Quote timing depends on underwriting.

Can I save or print my answers?

Yes. After submitting, use Print / Save PDF on the confirmation page. Select Save as PDF or your printer in the browser’s print window. Save your copy before leaving the page.

Does submitting bind coverage?

No. This is a quote request. Coverage requires underwriting approval and confirmation that it has been bound before it takes effect.
