Coverage Review

Coverage Options We Help You Review

Coverage is not identical across landlord and commercial property policies. I’ll review what is offered, the limits, and the exclusions for each available option.

  • ◆
    Building ProtectionProtection for covered damage to the building. Covered causes of loss, valuation, and deductibles depend on the policy selected.
  • ◆
    Other StructuresDetached garages, sheds, fences, or other structures may need separate limits or to be specifically scheduled.
  • ◆
    Landlord-Owned Personal PropertyCoverage options for appliances and furnishings you own at the rental. Your tenant’s belongings generally require renters insurance.
  • ◆
    Loss of Rents / Rental IncomeCoverage may help replace lost rental income after a covered property loss, subject to the policy’s terms, limits, and applicable time period.
  • ◆
    Premises LiabilityWhen included or purchased, liability coverage may respond to covered bodily injury or property damage claims. I’ll review the limit and exclusions.
  • ◆
    Medical PaymentsMay be available for eligible injury expenses, subject to policy terms. This coverage is not automatically included in every option.

The Coverage Form Matters

DP1 and DP2 generally cover specified causes of loss. DP3 generally provides broader protection for the dwelling unless a cause is excluded. That does not mean every loss is covered or every item is settled at replacement cost.

For apartment buildings and other eligible risks, commercial property forms may be the appropriate option. I’ll review the form that fits your property and explain any important limitations.

Additional Options to Discuss

Water backup, equipment breakdown, umbrella or excess liability, vacancy options, and portfolio programs may be available. Eligibility and terms vary by market and property.

Know What You’re Buying

What I Review Before You Buy

The premium is only one part of the decision. These details help you understand how a policy could respond when you need it.

Rebuilding Cost & Building Limits

Review the cost to rebuild, which can differ from purchase price or market value, along with applicable valuation and coinsurance terms.

Roof Settlement

Check whether roof losses are settled at replacement cost, actual cash value, or under a roof payment schedule, and review any restrictions.

Deductibles

Compare the amount you would pay toward a covered claim, including any separate wind, hail, or hurricane deductible.

Water Coverage

Review water damage limits, exclusions, and available water backup options. Discuss any separate flood coverage needs.

Vacancy & Renovation

Confirm how occupancy changes or construction work affect eligibility and coverage, including any notice requirements.

Lender & Ownership Details

Review the property owner or LLC name, lender information, and any coverage requirements provided by your lender.

Common Questions

Landlord Insurance FAQ

Do I need different insurance when I rent out my home?

Standard homeowners coverage is generally intended for an owner-occupied home. Tell me how the property will be used so I can review landlord coverage or other appropriate options with the market.

Can you review apartment buildings or multiple properties?

Yes. I review single-family rentals, small multifamily properties, apartment buildings, and portfolios. The available coverage form and program depend on the buildings, occupancy, and market eligibility.

What if my property is vacant or being renovated?

Include that in your request. Vacancy and renovation can change eligibility or restrict coverage under an existing policy. I’ll review options for the property’s current use and the work being performed.

What information do I need to start?

Have your contact information, property address, occupancy, roof age if known, insurance history, and desired start date ready. Select “Not sure” where available. You can email your current policy or supporting documents after submitting.

How quickly will I hear back?

The website sends an acknowledgment email after a successful submission, plus a confirmation text if you opt in. I review requests during business hours. A completed quote may require additional information or underwriting review.

Can I save a copy of my request?

Yes. After submitting, use Print / Save PDF on the confirmation page. Choose Save as PDF in your browser’s print window or select your printer. Save your copy before leaving the page.

Is coverage active after I submit the form?

No. The form is a quote request. Coverage is subject to underwriting approval and must be confirmed as bound before it takes effect.
