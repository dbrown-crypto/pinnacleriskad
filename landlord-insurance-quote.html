Coverage Options

Coverages We Help Review

Every rental property and landlord situation is different. We help you understand your coverage options and review what markets may offer for your specific property type and location.

🏠
DP1 & DP3 Dwelling Coverage

Dwelling fire policies (DP1 basic form and DP3 special form) for rental property structures.

💰
Loss of Rents / Loss of Income

Coverage for lost rental income when a covered loss makes your property temporarily uninhabitable.

⚖️
Landlord Liability

Liability protection for bodily injury or property damage claims arising from your rental property.

🏛
Vacant Property Coverage

Specialized coverage for unoccupied homes, properties between tenants, or homes under renovation.

📜
Lender-Required Coverage

Coverage review to help ensure your policy meets your mortgage lender or lienholder requirements.

🔭
Umbrella & Excess Liability

Additional liability limits above your base policy for landlords seeking broader protection.

Before You Begin

What We Need to Quote Your Rental Property

Gathering these items ahead of time will help us provide the most accurate quote. You don't need everything—share what you have and we'll follow up if anything else is needed.

🏠
Property Address

Full address of the rental property, including city and state.

📋
Property Type & Occupancy

Whether the property is a single-family rental, duplex, vacant, or under renovation.

🏭
Roof Age & Construction

Year the roof was last replaced and roof material, if known. Carriers often ask.

📈
Current Coverage

A copy of your current declarations page, if you have one, helps us understand your existing limits.

🔍
Prior Losses

Any property insurance claims in the last three to five years, including type and amount paid.

🏢
Lender Requirements

If you have a mortgage, your lender may require specific coverage limits or loss payee language.
