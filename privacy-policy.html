Privacy Policy

Pinnacle Risk Advisors LLC

Effective Date: January 31, 2026

Pinnacle Risk Advisors LLC (“Pinnacle,” “we,” “us,” “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you may have.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Company name and business details
  • Coverage preferences and insurance needs
  • Communications with us (email, phone, SMS)

Information Collected Automatically

Our website may collect limited technical information automatically (for example, browser type, device type, and basic usage analytics) to help maintain and improve the Site.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide quotes, policy services, and insurance support
  • Respond to inquiries and requests
  • Communicate about your quote, policy, or service needs
  • Improve website functionality and user experience
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

3. SMS/Text Messaging (TCPA)

Consent: If you check the SMS consent box on our forms, you expressly consent to receive text messages from Pinnacle Risk Advisors LLC at the number provided. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

  • Message frequency: varies based on your interaction with us
  • Rates: message and data rates may apply
  • Opt out: reply STOP to unsubscribe
  • Help: reply HELP or contact us directly
  • Eligibility: you must be 18+ to consent

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Insurance carriers for quote and policy purposes
  • Service providers that support our operations (e.g., form processing)
  • Legal authorities when required by law

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Request access to the personal information we maintain about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion (subject to legal/retention requirements)
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time

7. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies for basic functionality and analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date indicates the most recent revision.
