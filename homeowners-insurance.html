The Fine Print That Pays

Four things in your policy nobody explained

Direct quotes compete on one number, so they default to whatever configuration prices lowest. These are the settings that decide what a claim actually pays.

  • ◆
    Roof: replacement cost vs. actual cash valueAn ACV roof settlement deducts depreciation. On a 15-year-old roof, that can turn a $30,000 replacement into a check that does not come close. This single setting matters more than your premium.
  • ◆
    The percentage wind/hail deductibleNot a flat $1,000: a percentage of your dwelling limit. On a $400,000 home, a 2% wind deductible means the first $8,000 of a storm claim is yours. It is on page four, and almost nobody reads it.
  • ◆
    Water backup: usually not includedSewer and drain backup is one of the most common losses in metro Atlanta and one of the most commonly excluded. It is a cheap endorsement that most quote engines quietly leave off.
  • ◆
    Ordinance & lawAfter a major loss, the county makes you rebuild to current code, and the upgrade cost is not covered unless this endorsement says so. On older homes it is not optional in any real sense.

Common Questions

Homeowners insurance FAQ

My carrier non-renewed me. Can you actually help?

Often, yes. Non-renewals are frequently about carrier appetite: a company shrinking its exposure in your county rather than your specific house. A different market may write it normally. Send us the non-renewal notice and your dec page, and we will give you a straight answer quickly.

My roof is over 15 years old. Is that a problem?

It narrows the market, but it rarely closes it. The bigger issue is how the policy settles the roof: some carriers move older roofs to actual cash value automatically. We will tell you which of our markets still offers replacement cost for your roof’s age and material.

Do you write homeowners in Florida?

Case-by-case, honestly. Georgia is our primary homeowners market. For Florida properties, placement depends on the specific risk. Location, roof, wind mitigation, claims history, and we tell you within about a day whether we have a home for it rather than stringing you along.

I’m closing on a house. How fast can you move?

Fast. Lender binders are routine here. Send the address, the closing date, and your lender’s information, and we will have options and evidence of insurance in time for closing.

What do you need for a homeowners quote?

The property address, roughly when the roof was last replaced, and ideally your current declarations page. The dec page is the big one. It answers most underwriting questions in one shot and lets us compare coverage honestly instead of price against price.

Should I bundle my auto with my home?

Usually, yes. The multi-policy discount is typically the largest single discount on both policies, and having both with one advisor means your liability limits coordinate, which matters the day you add an umbrella. We quote it both ways and show you the difference.
