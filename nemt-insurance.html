Coverage Overview

Coverage for Your NEMT Operation

The program depends on your vehicles, people and contracts. Availability and coverage are subject to insurer approval and policy terms.

Commercial Auto Liability

Liability protection for covered bodily injury and property damage arising from insured vehicles. Limits should reflect your operation and current contracts.

Physical Damage

Comprehensive and collision for scheduled vehicles. Review vehicle values, wheelchair conversions, lifts, ramps and deductibles with us.

General Liability

Coverage for eligible non-auto business exposures, subject to policy terms. Passenger assistance and loading exposures need careful review across policies.

Workers’ Compensation

Review employee and attendant duties, payroll, state requirements and contract requirements when selecting coverage.

Hired & Non-Owned Auto

May address qualifying rented, borrowed or employee-owned vehicle liability. Passenger transport use must be disclosed and accepted by the insurer.

Umbrella & Excess Liability

Additional limits may be available over approved underlying policies when your transportation contracts require them.

Certificates & Contract Requirements

Send the insurance section of your current agreement. We review requested limits and endorsements before submission. Certificates reflect bound coverage; additional insured requests must be supported by the policy.

Understanding Your Premium

What Affects NEMT Insurance Cost?

A useful quote starts with the actual exposure. These factors help underwriters assess your operation, rather than relying on a generic price per van.

Vehicles & Equipment

Vehicle count, values, seating, wheelchair conversions and stretcher equipment.

Drivers & Experience

Driver records, license history, passenger transportation experience and management background.

Mileage & Territory

Annual miles, trips per day, operating radius and where vehicles are principally garaged.

Passengers & Assistance

Ambulatory, wheelchair and stretcher mix, attendants and the assistance your team provides.

Business & Loss History

Years operating, prior coverage, claims frequency and severity, and loss runs where available.

Limits & Contracts

Liability limits, deductibles, required endorsements and the transportation partners you serve.

Common Questions

NEMT Insurance Questions, Answered

Practical guidance for providers evaluating coverage and preparing a submission.

Can a brand-new NEMT company get insurance?

Potentially. New ventures are considered subject to carrier appetite and underwriting. Management experience, driver history, vehicle details, territory and passenger services matter when there is no operating loss history.

How much does NEMT insurance cost?

There is no single dependable flat rate. Vehicles, drivers, garaging territory, mileage, passenger mix, loss history, limits and contracts affect premium. A complete submission lets us seek terms for your actual operation.

What insurance does an NEMT company need?

Commercial auto liability is usually the foundation. Physical damage, general liability, workers’ compensation, hired and non-owned auto, and excess liability may also be appropriate depending on your business and contracts.

Can you insure wheelchair vans and lifts?

Eligible wheelchair operations can be submitted to available markets. Provide vehicle values and conversion details. Coverage for lifts, ramps and other equipment must be confirmed in the policy; it is not automatic.

Can you insure non-emergency stretcher transportation?

Certain markets may consider it. Describe attendants, equipment and any medical services provided. Emergency ambulance and 911 response are separate exposures and are outside this page’s scope.

Can I insure mixed ambulatory and wheelchair trips?

Mixed operations may be eligible. Give us realistic percentages for each passenger service, including any stretcher work, so the submission accurately describes the business.

What insurance limits does Verida require?

Send your current provider agreement and insurance requirements. Limits and endorsements should be reviewed against the exact contract and operation rather than a generic figure online.

Do NEMT companies need workers’ compensation?

Employee structure, state law and contracts determine the requirement. Tell us about drivers, attendants and payroll so we can review the coverage needed.

Is NEMT insurance the same as trucking insurance?

No. NEMT transports passengers and is underwritten differently from freight trucking. Your insurance application needs to describe medical transportation accurately.

Does Pinnacle write NEMT in Georgia and Florida?

Pinnacle is licensed in both states and can submit eligible NEMT operations to available markets, subject to underwriting and carrier appetite.

Can Pinnacle issue certificates of insurance?

After coverage is bound, we can issue certificates that accurately reflect the policy. Additional insured status and special wording must be supported by the policy and applicable endorsements. A certificate does not expand coverage.

What do I need to start a quote?

Start with your company, contact information, state, vehicle count and operation type. We then collect vehicle and driver schedules, insurance history, operational details and contract requirements through follow-up.

Do my drivers need a commercial license?

License requirements depend on vehicle specifications, seating capacity, use and applicable rules. We review driver and vehicle information for underwriting; confirm licensing obligations with the relevant licensing authority.

Can you help after another carrier declined my operation?

Possibly. Market appetite varies. Share the operation details and the reason for the decline so we can assess which available markets may consider the risk.
