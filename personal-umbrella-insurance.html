The Math Nobody Shows You

Where your current limits run out

This is the whole argument for umbrella coverage, and it takes about thirty seconds to understand.

  • ◆
    Your auto liability limit is a ceiling, not a promiseIf you carry 250/500 and cause an accident with serious injuries, the carrier pays up to the limit and then stops. The rest of the judgment does not disappear. It follows you.
  • ◆
    A judgment can reach past your insuranceDepending on the state and the circumstances, that can mean savings, investment accounts, equity, and in many cases future wages through garnishment. This is the part people do not picture.
  • ◆
    Your homeowners liability has the same ceilingA guest injured at your house, a dog bite, a teenager’s party. All of it runs through a limit that was probably set once and never revisited.
  • ◆
    Umbrella sits on top of bothOne policy, extending over your auto and home liability, that picks up where those limits end.
  • ◆
    It is priced per million, and the first million is the cheapestUmbrella is one of the few places in insurance where the coverage is large and the premium is small, because catastrophic claims are rare.

What an umbrella typically requires

Umbrella carriers will not sit on top of limits they consider too thin. Most require underlying auto liability and homeowners liability at specified minimums before they will write over them.

That is why umbrella is easiest when your auto and home are handled by the same agency. If your underlying limits need to come up, that adjustment is usually far cheaper than people expect, and sometimes the umbrella plus the higher underlying limits costs less than what a separate agency was already charging.

We will tell you what your current policies would need to look like before we quote you anything.

Who Needs One Most

If any of these describe you, you are exposed

Umbrella is not about how much money you have. It is about how much a claim could take.

●

You own rental property

Rentals multiply your liability surface: tenants, their guests, stairs, pools, dogs, parking lots. Investors are among the most underinsured umbrella prospects we see. Coverage over rental exposure varies by carrier, so this is worth an actual conversation.

●

You run a business

Business owners are perceived as having assets, and plaintiff attorneys act accordingly. Your commercial policy protects the business. Your personal umbrella protects you.

●

You have a teen driver

The single largest jump in household liability risk, and it arrives overnight. Umbrella is the cheapest way to respond to it.

●

You have a pool, trampoline, or dog

All three are standard liability triggers. Homeowners liability responds up to a limit that was probably chosen by default years ago.

●

You have real equity or future earnings

Umbrella protects what you have built and what you are going to earn. Younger high earners often have more to lose in future wages than in current savings.

●

You have never had it reviewed

Most people set their liability limits once, at the original purchase, and never look again. Meanwhile the assets grew and the limits did not.

Common Questions

Umbrella insurance FAQ

How much does a personal umbrella cost?

Less than most people assume. Umbrella is generally one of the lowest-cost-per-dollar-of-coverage policies available, because catastrophic claims are rare. Actual pricing depends on your drivers, vehicles, properties, claim history, and the limit you choose, so we quote it rather than publishing a number that would be wrong for half the people reading it. Call and we will give you a real figure for your household.

Do I have to move my auto and home to you to buy an umbrella?

Not always, but often it is simpler and cheaper. Many umbrella carriers prefer or require writing the underlying policies. We will tell you upfront which of your options need the auto and home to move and which do not.

Does my personal umbrella cover my rental properties?

Sometimes, and it depends entirely on the carrier and how the properties are owned and titled. Some personal umbrellas will extend over a limited number of rental dwellings if they are scheduled; others exclude them and the right answer is a commercial umbrella instead. This is exactly the kind of detail worth getting right in advance rather than at claim time. Tell us what you own and we will confirm it in writing.

What does an umbrella not cover?

An umbrella is liability coverage. It does not pay to repair your own car or your own house. It also generally excludes intentional acts, business activities not tied to a covered policy, and certain vehicles or watercraft unless they are specifically scheduled. We will walk the exclusions with you before you bind.

How much umbrella coverage should I carry?

The common rule of thumb is at least enough to cover your net worth, and many advisors add expected future earnings on top of that. We will walk through what you actually own and what a judgment could realistically reach, then size it from there.

I already have an umbrella. Is it worth reviewing?

Yes, particularly if it has been in place for several years. The two things that go stale are the limit, which rarely keeps pace with what you have accumulated, and the underlying policies, which may have changed carriers or limits since the umbrella was written.
