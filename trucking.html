Coverage Lines

Everything You Need to Roll

A complete trucking insurance program covers multiple lines. Here's what we write — and what each one does for you.

FMCSA Required

Primary Auto Liability

Covers bodily injury and property damage you cause to others while operating your CMV. Required by the FMCSA to obtain your operating authority — minimum $750K for general freight, $1M for hazmat. We file the MCS-90 endorsement on your behalf.

Shipper Required

Motor Truck Cargo

Covers the freight you're hauling in case of theft, accident, fire, or other covered loss. Most shippers and brokers require $100K minimum. We can write limits up to the value of the loads you pull — essential for high-value freight.

Physical Damage

Comprehensive and collision coverage for your truck and trailer. Covers your equipment when it's damaged in an accident, stolen, vandalized, or hit by weather. Lenders require it for financed equipment — but it's smart regardless.

Bobtail / Non-Trucking Liability

Covers you when you're operating your truck without a load — driving bobtail, deadheading, or using the truck for personal use outside of dispatch. Critical for owner-operators who are not under dispatch 100% of the time.

General Liability (Trucking)

Covers premises and operations liability — incidents that happen away from the truck, like loading dock accidents, office liability, or completed operations. Required by some contracts and shippers even when auto liability is in place.

Occupational Accident

For owner-operators and independent contractors not covered under workers' comp — occ/acc provides benefits for on-the-job injuries, disability, and accidental death. A cost-effective alternative to WC for 1099 operators.

Starting Your Operating Authority?

We help new operators get the insurance filings needed to activate their FMCSA operating authority — fast.

FMCSA Minimum Requirements

The FMCSA sets minimum insurance requirements based on what you haul. Here are the standard thresholds for most general freight operations:

  • General Freight — Primary Liability$750,000
  • Hazardous Materials$1,000,000+
  • Household Goods$300,000
  • For-Hire Passenger (bus)$5,000,000
  • MCS-90 FilingIncluded
