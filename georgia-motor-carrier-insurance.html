What a motor carrier policy can include

The right package depends on what you haul, where you run, your contracts and the equipment you want protected.

Auto Liability

Liability protection for covered bodily injury and property damage arising from operation of insured commercial vehicles. Limits depend on the operation and contractual requirements.

Motor Truck Cargo

Coverage for covered loss or damage to freight in your care, custody or control, subject to policy terms, exclusions, limits and deductibles.

Physical Damage

Comprehensive and collision protection for scheduled tractors and other covered equipment, generally based on stated or agreed values and the selected deductible.

Trailer Interchange

Can protect trailers you do not own while they are in your possession under a written trailer interchange agreement when the policy includes the coverage.

General Liability

Addresses certain premises and operations exposures outside commercial auto. Contracts may require this separately from primary auto liability.

Certificates and Filings

After binding, Pinnacle helps with certificate requests supported by the policy. Required federal insurance filings are made according to carrier and FMCSA procedures.

Georgia motor carrier questions

Frequently asked questions

How much is motor carrier insurance in Georgia?

Premium depends on drivers, equipment, cargo, radius, limits, operating history and losses. A complete submission is required for an accurate quote.

What liability limits does the FMCSA require?

Federal requirements vary by operation and cargo. Contracts and brokers may require higher limits than the minimum applicable to the carrier.

Do you handle MCS 90 and BOC 3 filings?

When required, the insurer handles federal evidence of insurance filings and the MCS 90 endorsement. A process agent, not the insurance agency, files the BOC 3.

How fast can I get a certificate of insurance?

After coverage is bound, certificates can be requested subject to the policy and any endorsement requirements. Timing depends on the request.

Can a new venture get covered?

Some markets consider new ventures. Driver quality, equipment, cargo, radius and the authority plan determine available options.

What does motor truck cargo cover?

It can cover specified loss or damage to freight in your care, custody or control, subject to the policy's terms, exclusions, deductible and limit.
